Your Spotify listening

Import your listening history

Spotify's live data only covers fixed windows: about 4 weeks, 6 months and a year. For all-time stats and any date range you pick, add the full history Spotify keeps for you:

  1. On your Spotify privacy settings page, under Download your data, tick Extended streaming history and request it.
  2. Confirm the email from Spotify. They send a download link when it's ready, usually within a few days (it can take up to 30).
  3. Drop the ZIP file here. It's read in this browser and never uploaded, and the page remembers it for next time.
